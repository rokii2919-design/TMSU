# The Myriad Sea Universe
## Episode 1 - The Nameless Traveler

Ray arrives at Seven Winds Market, a floating city above a golden sea of clouds.

The True Name Registry fails to read his existence.

Moments later, a Nameless Shadow attacks a child.

Ray manifests Noctis Null, a black void sword born from the resonance of emptiness.

Park Arin witnesses the impossible.

A forbidden gate begins to open beyond the clouds.
